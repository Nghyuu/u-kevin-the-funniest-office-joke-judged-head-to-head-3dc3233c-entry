# Reply-All Intervention

One short line about office life, written for the make.fun bounty
[The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c).

The joke turns on the reply-all chain: the one person who tries to stop
the flood becomes the flood, and the punchline lands last — HR wants a
word about their tone. A setup anyone who has worked in an office
recognises, and a turn the reader does not see coming.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the single joke line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and chmods it to 0644.
